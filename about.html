Company Overview

Who We Are

Dynatec Industrial Solutions Ltd is a Kenyan-based engineering and industrial solutions company headquartered in Ruiru, along Thika Superhighway.

We serve clients across Kenya and East Africa in industries such as manufacturing, construction, mining, energy, logistics, healthcare, and government infrastructure.

Our core expertise includes:

  • • Mechanical engineering services
  • • Electrical engineering solutions
  • • Industrial maintenance and repairs
  • • Engineering project management
  • • Industrial safety systems
  • • PPE supply and safety equipment

We are committed to delivering high-quality engineering solutions that meet international standards while addressing local industrial challenges.
